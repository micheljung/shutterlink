Shutterlink

Get the photos off your camera and onto your phone, back them up to your own server as you shoot, and hand the people you photograph their pictures before they walk away.

Download for Android Source code

What it does

Browse & Download

See everything on the memory card, select a range or a whole day, and save the shots to your phone. RAW and JPEG, exactly as the camera wrote them. Interrupted? Resume where it stopped.

Auto Upload

Every new shot goes straight to your own storage while you keep shooting: Nextcloud, WebDAV, S3-compatible storage, SFTP or Immich. It keeps going in the background.

Live Share

Let the person you photograph scan a QR code. Their photos appear in their own web gallery seconds after you take them. No app and no account for them.

Screenshots

Private by design

No account, no cloud you don't own, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. Your photos go only where you point them. Open source under the MIT license. Read the privacy policy.

Supported cameras

The iPhone app builds but has not been tried with a real camera yet. Reports from other cameras are very welcome: open an issue.

Get it

  • GitHub Releases: signed APK. Obtainium can follow the repository for updates.
  • Google Play and F-Droid: planned. Builds from different stores are signed with different keys, so switching between them means uninstalling once.
  • Self-host the Sessions server for Live Share: a small Docker image, ghcr.io/micheljung/shutterlink-server.
