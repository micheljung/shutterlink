Shutterlink downloads JPEG and CR3 photos from a Canon EOS R to your phone over the camera's Wi-Fi. It talks to the camera directly using PTP/IP. No cloud of ours, no account, no Canon app. Optionally upload to your own WebDAV, S3 or SFTP server, or to Immich.

Features:
* Thumbnail grid of the memory card, grouped by day. A RAW+JPEG pair is one tile.
* Download as JPEG, RAW (CR3) or both. Files are saved byte-identical to the originals.
* Downloaded photos are marked and skipped on later downloads. "Select new" selects everything not yet downloaded.
* Full-screen preview with swipe. Choose thumbnail or full-resolution quality in Settings.
* Select a whole day, or long-press and drag to select a range.
* Interrupted downloads resume after reconnecting.
* Auto-upload (Android): Live transfer sends new photos to your server while the app runs in the background.
* Connects in the camera's access point mode, and finds the camera on your home Wi-Fi.

Shutterlink is not tested on real hardware yet. Reports are welcome.
